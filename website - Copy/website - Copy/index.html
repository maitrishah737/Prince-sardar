About

The edit is where the story actually happens.

I've spent seven years as a video editor and creative professional, cutting cinematic edits, high-converting ads and social content that people finish watching. My focus is visual storytelling and creative strategy — the pacing, the sound, the frame you choose to hold on.

I founded Dream Media in 2024 to take briefs end to end: script, edit, grade, motion, final delivery. Before that, and still now, I work directly with brands and creators across the USA, Australia and Europe. I'm based in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, and work remote.
